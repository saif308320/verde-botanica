Founder Story

"I wanted a serum I could hand to my grandmother and to my teenage niece, and trust it would be right for both."

Elena Moretti Founder & Formulator

Verde Botanica started in a rented Milan kitchen in 2017, where Elena hand-mixed the first batch of what became our Rosewater Cleansing Balm. The goal was simple: formulas honest enough to read the ingredient list aloud.

Nearly a decade on, we still work directly with the small farms that grow our botanicals, and every formula is still tested in-house before it reaches a single shelf.
